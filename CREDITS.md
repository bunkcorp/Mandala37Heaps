# Credits

**37 Heaps Mandala** is an original educational visionOS experience inspired by the traditional Tibetan Buddhist practice of the 37-heap mandala offering.

- Game design, procedural RealityKit scene, and SwiftUI UI: original work for this project.
- Heap names follow commonly taught traditional mandala offering enumerations (Mount Meru, four continents, subcontinents, precious substances, offering goddesses, sun/moon, parasol, victory banner).
- Physical layout uses a **stacked four-ring** offering-set structure: finish each level to place the next smaller ring, then crown the mandala with a top ornament.
- No third-party 3D models or audio assets are bundled.

This app is intended for contemplative play and learning; it is not a substitute for instruction from a qualified teacher.
